A family knitwear business, still run from the factory floor.

Panwar Knitwear was founded in Ludhiana — India's knitwear capital — and has grown into a manufacturer and exporter serving wholesalers, retail labels and export buyers.

Our story

Started by one family, built around repeat customers.

The business was founded by Mohar Singh Panwar and is run today by Prabhu Panwar and Bhala Ram Panwar, with Rohitash Panwar leading online operations and buyer communication.

What began as a small knitwear unit now covers both halves of the wardrobe: ZONIXA for top wear and MSP Sports for bottom wear. Most of our volume comes from buyers who return every season, which is why we would rather quote an honest timeline than an optimistic one.

How we work

Four things we hold to on every order.

01

Honest timelines

02
