From yarn to packed carton, in one unit.

Knitting, dyeing and finishing, cutting, stitching, printing, embroidery, checking and packing are coordinated in-house — which is what keeps quality and delivery dates predictable across a bulk order.

Process

Six stages every order passes through

Each stage has a sign-off before the order moves forward, so a problem is caught at the stage it happened rather than at final packing.

  1. Knitting

    Fabric knitted to the GSM and construction approved on your sample — fleece, terry, single jersey, honeycomb and more.

  2. Dyeing & finishing

    Shade matched against an approved swatch, then finished for handle, shrinkage and colour fastness.

  3. Cutting

    Layered and cut to the graded pattern, with panels bundled by size to keep shade and measurement consistent.

  4. Stitching

    Assembly on a line set up for the style, with inline checks on seams, cuffs, hoods and waistbands.

  5. Printing & embroidery

    Your artwork applied in-house — placement, size and wash durability confirmed on a sample first.

  6. Checking & packing

    Measurement, finish and trims checked against spec, then packed and labelled to your ratio and carton marking.
